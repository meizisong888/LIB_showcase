# Access and sharing

Status recorded September 27, 2026.

## Publicly documented platform access

The [official HokieAI overview](https://ai.vt.edu/tools/hokieai.html) describes personal agent creation and sharing and identifies students, faculty, and staff as the audience. The [VT access article](https://4help.vt.edu/sp?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0016390) says eligible employees and students sign in with VT credentials; it does not extend access to guests or alumni.

The [AAD IT guide](https://it.aad.vt.edu/ai/tools/doing-more/) states that official agents are reviewed and managed by a department, service owner, or administrator and that individuals are not authorized to create official agents. This is separate from the unresolved sharing controls for the owner's Personal Agent.
